### Qi et al. 2026
[T2VEval: Benchmark dataset and objective evaluation method for
T2V-generated videos](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S014193822500215X)

- Design choice:
    - The creators of this project wanted to create a T2V assessment model that isn't just based on one score but evaluates different metrics to give a more accurate assessment of the generated video. Previous models typically only used a single score to measure the effectiveness of the T2V model. This can ultimately end up obscuring the strengths and weaknesses of the model's performance on certain tasks. By breaking up the initial training of the evaluation model into different dimensions, the final assessment score will more accurately reflect the quality of the video generated by the T2V model. 
    - They chose to evaluate four different dimensions: overall impression, **text-video consistency**, **realness**, and **technical quality**. 

- Implementation note:
    - The authors of this paper chose to evaluate overall impressions by having 73 evaluators judge the quality of their test videos. 
    - Realness, text-video consistency, and technical quality were all evaluated using individual networks. 
        - Technical quality was evaluated using the Swin-3d network.
        - Realness was evaluated by using the ConvLexNet-3D network.
        - Text-video alignment was evaluated using the BLIP network.
    - After each branch has been evaluated, they are sent into a fusion unit. Then they are sent to an LLM to predict the assessment score for the video. 

- Evaluation results:
    - The model proposed in this paper overall outperformed other T2V video assessment models. 
    - When determining the effectiveness of this model, removing any one of the branches led to a decrease in the model's accuracy in predicting an accuracy score. Technical quality overall had the least effect on the overall model's accuracy, meaning that as we determine how to test and evaluate our model we should focus on video-consistency and realness first. 
    - Due to the "divide and conquer" approach (having the separate models for each dimension) the model was able to more accurately evaluate the quality of the T2V videos when compared with other assessment models.

- My Notes:
    - This paper provided a very interesting insight into how to evaluate the quality of AI generated videos. 
    - The "divide and conquer" approach works well with our plans to utilize agents for different parts of T2V development. If we chose to implement this structure, or something similar, we could possibly utilize different agents to evaluate each dimension for accuracy before combining the results together into one final score. 

### Liu et al. 2026
[MTPA: A multi-aspects perception assisted AIGV quality assessment model](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1047320326000167)

- Design choice:
    - This paper proposes an assisted AI generated video (AIGV) quality assessment model called MTPA. This model focuses on analyzing the visual perceptual and text-to-video alignment of the AIGV. The goal of evaluating these features is to examine the spatial and temporal quality of the generated videos. Like the model presented in Qi et al, this model utilizes a fusion model to combine branching models into one quality score. This model primarily focuses on capturing accurate information regarding text-video alignment and visual spatial perception and creates a quality score based on those two parameters. 

- Implementation note:
    - This model implements three features to calculate the final evaluation score: text-video alignment score, visual spatial perceptual score, and visual temporal perceptual score. Together these scores combine in a fusion model to return a quality evaluation of an AIGV.
    - To conduct their experiments, the researchers used the LGVQ, MTQ, and T2VQA datasets. They compared their proposed MTPA model with 5 IQA (image quality assessment) models, 6 VQA (video quality assessment) models, 3 CLIP based models, and 4 text-to-image alignment models. 
- Evaluation results:
    - When compared with the performance of the other models the MTPA outperformed all of them. In each dataset that the MTPA model was tested, it more accurately evaluated the text-video alignment and visual spatial perception compared to the other models. When it comes to determining the quality of AI generated videos, it is best to evaluate the dimensions of the video separately before combining into one quality score. 
- My Notes: 
    - Dividing up the evaluations based on dimension seems to be the most effective and accurate way to test the quality of an AI generated video. When we start to look at how we can evaluate our model, we should keep in mind that these assessment models perform better when they are specialized and then brought together through a fusion model. 
    - If we use a model like the one proposed in this paper to assess our video quality, we can use agents to supervise each part of the evaluation process to keep things running smoothly. We could assign one agent to test visual perception and another to test text-to-video alignment. 
    - Text-to-video alignment seems to be one of the most important features that we will need to test when evaluating the quality of our generated videos. We should definitely think about including some way of assessing that feature in our project.
